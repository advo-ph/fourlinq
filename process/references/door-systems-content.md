| `name` | Lift & Slide |
| `shortDescription` | Effortless sliding on large, heavy panels. |
| `description` | Two-panel sliding door with a lift-and-slide mechanism. Rotating the handle lifts the panel off its compression seal, so even a large, heavy panel moves with minimal effort.
